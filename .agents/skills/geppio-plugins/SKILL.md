Trace durable installation, accepted generation, active instance, view and ordinary data separately. At each boundary identify the supervised caller, validated serializable payload, permission, cancellation and revocation behavior. Nested contributions retain their contributor identity.

Exercise supported v1/v2 fixtures when changing public contracts. Check stale references, denied operations, unload and reload, and candidate rejection preserving the old snapshot. Test signed package integrity against authorized roots with exact content.

Measure original-folder edit and Atualizar through usable activation. Preserve developer data and distinguish prepublication failures from postactivation failures. Document the threat model supported by the mechanism actually tested.
